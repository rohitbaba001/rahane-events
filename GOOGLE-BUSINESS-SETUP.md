## Step 10: Get Reviews (MOST IMPORTANT!)

### How to Get Reviews:
1. After each event, send customers this link:
   ```
   https://g.page/r/[YOUR_BUSINESS_ID]/review
   ```
   (Get this from your Google Business Profile)

2. Send via WhatsApp:
   ```
   Thank you for choosing Caterer's & Catering Services! 
   We hope you enjoyed our service. 
   Please share your experience: [review link]
   ```

3. **Goal**: Get 20+ reviews in first 3 months
4. **Target Rating**: 4.5+ stars

### Responding to Reviews:
- Respond to EVERY review within 24 hours
- Thank positive reviewers
- Address negative reviews professionally
- Never argue or be defensive

2. ❌ No photos or old photos
3. ❌ Not responding to reviews
4. ❌ Inconsistent business hours
5. ❌ Wrong category selection
6. ❌ Not posting regularly
7. ❌ Ignoring Q&A section
8. ❌ Not asking for reviews
